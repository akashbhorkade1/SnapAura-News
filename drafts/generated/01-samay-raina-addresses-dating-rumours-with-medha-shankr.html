Samay Raina Addresses Dating Rumours With Medha Shankr

SnapAura bollywood Desk – 2026-09-06

In recent entertainment news reported by NDTV, stand-up comedian and digital creator Samay Raina addressed ongoing public curiosity regarding his relationship with actress Medha Shankr. During an episode of his popular digital series, "India's Got Latent 2," Raina publicly described Shankr as his "sabse achi dost"—a Hindi phrase translating to "best friend." The statement was made directly in response to widespread online rumors and media speculation suggesting that the two figures were romantically involved.

The remark has drawn notable attention across social media platforms and digital news outlets. For weeks prior to the broadcast, fans and online commentators had actively engaged in speculation about a potential romantic connection between the comedian and the actress. According to reporting from NDTV, Raina's statement on "India's Got Latent 2" serves as an explicit effort to clarify the nature of their relationship, framing their bond strictly around close friendship rather than romantic ties.
