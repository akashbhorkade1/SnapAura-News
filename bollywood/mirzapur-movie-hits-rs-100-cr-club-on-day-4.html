Mirzapur Movie Hits Rs 100 Cr Club on Day 4

SnapAura bollywood Desk – 2026-09-08

In a significant theatrical milestone reported by NDTV, "Mirzapur The Movie" has officially entered the coveted Rs 100 crore club on its fourth day in theaters. The film, which prominently stars actor Ali Fazal, maintained strong audience engagement through its initial weekend and into the critical first Monday period. NDTV highlighted this rapid surge in box office earnings as a major achievement for the production, signaling robust interest from cinema audiences during its early opening run.

The achievement marks a swift progression for the film, reaching the three-figure crore mark within just four days of its nationwide release. According to NDTV's box office tracking update, the Monday collections provided the necessary boost to propel the movie past the Rs 100 crore threshold. First-Monday performance is traditionally regarded by film industry analysts as a vital indicator of a movie's overall theatrical sustainability and word-of-mouth momentum beyond the initial weekend surge.

Headline Milestone: Entry into the Rs 100 Crore Club

According to figures and headlines compiled by NDTV, the fourth day of theatrical release served as the pivotal moment for "Mirzapur The Movie" to cross the Rs 100 crore milestone. Achieving this status on a Monday—typically a day that sees a standard drop in ticket sales compared to weekend screenings—underscores the commercial pull of the property during its debut week.

NDTV reported that the film's strong box office trajectory on Day 4 reflects sustained attendance across key markets. While detailed morning-to-evening occupancy percentages or regional distributor shares were not fully itemized in the immediate report, NDTV confirmed that the cumulative total safely crossed the benchmark on its first Monday. The milestone underscores how high-profile properties can generate significant opening week momentum when entering mainstream theatrical venues.

Contextualizing the Franchise and Ali Fazal's Lead

The success reported by NDTV places actor Ali Fazal in a central spotlight for the feature project. As highlighted in NDTV's coverage, Fazal's association with the title remains a prominent draw for audiences tracking the cinematic evolution of the "Mirzapur" brand. The adaptation or expansion of established story worlds into standalone cinematic experiences represents an ongoing trend in the contemporary Indian film industry.

Contextually, the "Mirzapur" name carries widespread recognition stemming from its prior multi-season presence in popular media culture. Translating established narrative hype into physical ticket sales at the box office requires substantial market demand. NDTV's reporting indicates that the transition to big-screen exhibition has resonated with moviegoers, as evidenced by the rapid accumulation of gross collection totals within the first four days.

In theatrical trade analysis, a film's first Monday is universally monitored as a key test of long-term commercial viability. While weekend numbers often reflect pre-release anticipation, marketing reach, and advance bookings, weekday receipts indicate whether general audience interest remains stable. As NDTV observed, "Mirzapur The Movie" maintained sufficient ticket volume on Day 4 to push its total earnings into the Rs 100 crore range.

This rapid entry into the Rs 100 crore club within four days positions the film among notable high-performing releases of the season.
