SSB Constable Bharti 2026: 827 Vacancies

SnapAura Career Desk – March 23, 2026

Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB) announced recruitment for 827 Constable posts across 11 trades — including Driver (553), Veterinary (35), Tailor (41) and more. Applications closed on 20 April 2026; exam details are announced later on the official portal.

Can I Apply? 👀

Applications for this cycle closed on 20 April 2026. The eligibility that applied (per the official advertisement):

  • Education: 10th pass for all posts, plus trade-specific requirements.
  • Driver: Heavy Motor Vehicle (HMV) driving licence.
  • Trades 3–10: 2 years' experience, or ITI in the trade + 1 year, or an ITI diploma.
  • Veterinary: 1 year's experience; Nursing Orderly: First Aid certificate + 1 year in a 50-bed hospital.
  • Age: 18–27 (Veterinary, Driver, Nursing Orderly); 18–23 (Gardner to Waiter); 18–25 (Carpenter), as on 19 April 2026. SC/ST 5 years' relaxation, OBC 3 years.

Selection Process

Selection ran in stages: Physical Standard Test (PST) and Physical Efficiency Test (PET), a trade/skill test for posts like Driver, a written examination, document verification, and a final medical examination. Exact physical standards were given in the official notification PDF.

What should I do now? 🚀

  1. Applications are closed for this cycle — the next step is the exam/admit card stage.
  2. Track updates only on the official SSB recruitment portal.
  3. Keep your registration details and documents handy for verification.
  4. Prepare for PST/PET and the written exam using the official syllabus.

मराठीत समजून घ्या (Quick Marathi Guide)

SSB म्हणजेच सशस्त्र सीमा बल — भारताच्या सीमा भागात कार्यरत असलेले केंद्रीय सशस्त्र पोलीस दल. या भरतीमध्ये 11 विविध व्यवसाय ट्रेडमधील एकूण 827 कॉन्स्टेबल जागा होत्या, ज्यात ड्रायव्हर (553) सर्वाधिक जागांचा समावेश होता. अर्ज प्रक्रिया पूर्णपणे ऑनलाइन होती आणि अर्ज 20 एप्रिल 2026 रोजी बंद झाले. 10वी उत्तीर्ण उमेदवारांनाच अर्ज करता येत होता; ड्रायव्हरसाठी HMV परवाना तर इतर ट्रेडसाठी अनुभव किंवा ITI आवश्यक होता. निवड प्रक्रियेत PST/PET, ट्रेड टेस्ट, लेखी परीक्षा, कागदपत्र तपासणी व वैद्यकीय तपासणी होते. ही भरती बंद झाली असल्याने आता पुढील टप्प्याचे अपडेट — परीक्षा तारीख, प्रवेशपत्र व निकाल — फक्त अधिकृत संकेतस्थळावर पहावेत. पुढील भरतीसाठी तयारी करणाऱ्यांनी शारीरिक तयारी व लेखी परीक्षेचा अभ्यास सुरू ठेवावा.
