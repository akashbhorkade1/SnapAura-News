Delhi High Court Recruitment 2026: 150 PA & SPA Vacancies

SnapAura Career Desk – 2026-09-06

The High Court of Delhi has announced recruitment for 150 Senior Personal Assistant (SPA) and Personal Assistant (PA) posts under the Senior Personal Assistant & Personal Assistant Examination–2026. Online applications open 15 September 2026 and close 5 October 2026.

🏢 OrganizationHigh Court of Delhi
💼 PostsSenior Personal Assistant & Personal Assistant
🔢 Vacancies150 (SPA – 117, PA – 33)
🎓 QualificationGraduate + English shorthand + typing
🎂 Age Limit18–32 years (as on 01-01-2026)
💰 Application Fee₹1,500 (₹1,300 SC/ST/PWD)
📅 Last Date5 October 2026, 11:00 PM
📍 Job LocationNew Delhi

Can I Apply? 👀

Per the details reported by Majhi Naukri, you must meet all of these:

  • Education: Graduate degree from a recognised university.
  • Senior Personal Assistant: English shorthand speed of 110 wpm and computer typing speed of 40 wpm.
  • Personal Assistant: English shorthand speed of 100 wpm and computer typing speed of 40 wpm.
  • Age: 18 to 32 years as on 1 January 2026. SC/ST candidates get 5 years' relaxation and OBC candidates 3 years.

What should I do now? 🚀

  1. Check your shorthand and typing speed against the post requirements.
  2. Read the official notification (PDF) carefully.
  3. Keep your degree and category documents ready.
  4. Apply through the official Delhi High Court portal before the deadline.
