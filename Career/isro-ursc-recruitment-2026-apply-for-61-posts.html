ISRO URSC Recruitment 2026: Apply for 61 Posts

SnapAura Career Desk – 2026-10-25

🚀 ISRO URSC Recruitment 2026 Overview

U R Rao Satellite Centre (URSC), a constituent unit of the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO), is inviting online applications for 61 vacancies across Technician-B and Driver posts. Candidates with ITI/NTC/NAC qualifications or 10th pass with relevant driving experience can submit their applications online before the deadline on 13 November 2026. As reported by Majhi Naukri, the posts are located in Bengaluru, Karnataka.

🎓 Can I Apply?

Qualification Requirements:

  • Technician-B (51 Posts): Must possess ITI, NTC, or NAC certificate in the relevant trade (Electronics Mechanic, Fitter, Electrician, Refrigeration & AC, Turner, Machinist, Motor Vehicle Mechanic, or Electroplater).
  • Light Vehicle Driver-A (03 Posts) & Staff Car Driver-A (02 Posts): 10th pass with a valid Light Motor Vehicle (LMV) driving license and 3 years of experience.
  • Heavy Vehicle Driver-A (10 Posts): 10th pass with a valid Heavy Motor Vehicle (HMV) driving license and 5 years of experience.

Age Limit & Relaxations:

  • Candidates must be between 18 and 35 years of age as on 13 November 2026.
  • Upper age relaxation is available for reserved categories: SC/ST candidates get 5 years, and OBC candidates get 3 years.

Confirm your eligibility against the detailed criteria specified in the notification before applying.

💼 Why This Job?

  • Opportunity to work at URSC Bengaluru, a key ISRO facility for spacecraft design and manufacturing.
  • Clear recruitment process with partial or full refund of application fee upon appearing for the written examination.
  • Positions available for both technical ITI holders and experienced driver applicants.

🏢 Background Context

The U R Rao Satellite Centre (URSC), formerly known as the ISRO Satellite Centre (ISAC), was established in 1972 as the Indian Scientific Satellite Project in Bengaluru's Peenya Industrial Estates. As a lead facility under the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO), URSC is responsible for the design, development, and fabrication of spacecraft for communication, navigation, remote sensing, and space science missions.
