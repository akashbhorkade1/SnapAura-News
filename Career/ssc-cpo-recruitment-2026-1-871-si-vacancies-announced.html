SSC CPO 2026: 1,871 SI Vacancies — Eligibility & Last Date

SnapAura Career Desk – 2026-09-11

SSC has announced the Sub-Inspector in Delhi Police and Central Armed Police Forces (CAPF) Examination 2026 for 1,871 vacancies. Graduates can apply online until 30 September 2026, 11:00 PM.

🏢 OrganizationStaff Selection Commission (SSC)
💼 PostSub-Inspector (Delhi Police & CAPFs)
🔢 Vacancies1,871
🎓 QualificationDegree (post-specific, see below)
🎂 Age Limit20–25 / 18–30 years (as on 01-08-2026)
💰 Application Fee₹100 (Gen/OBC); SC/ST/ExSM exempt
📅 Last Date30 September 2026, 11:00 PM
📍 Job LocationAll India

Can I Apply? 👀

Per the notification (Advt. E/37/2026-C-2) as reported by Majhi Naukri:

🪫 Delhi Police SI – Male (205):any degree + valid Light Motor Vehicle (LMV) driving licence.
🪫 Delhi Police SI – Female (112) & CAPF SI GD (1,320):degree in any discipline.
🪫 CAPF SI Fire – Male (234):B.Sc. with Physics, Chemistry & Maths, OR 10th pass with an engineering diploma (Mechanical/Civil/Electrical/Automobile/Chemical/Mining/Aeronautical/Telecommunication).
🎂 Age:20–25 for Posts 1–3; 18–30 for Post 4, as on 1 August 2026. SC/ST 5 years' relaxation, OBC 3 years.

If you hold a degree (plus an LMV licence for Delhi Police Male) and meet the age limit for your post, apply online before 30 September 2026.

  • ☐ Age per post: 20–25 / 18–30 as on 1 August 2026

What should I do now? 🚀

  1. Match your degree (and LMV licence, for Delhi Police Male) to the post.
  2. Read the official notification (PDF) carefully.
  3. Keep your documents and photo/signature scans ready.
  4. Apply on the official SSC portal before 30 September 2026, 11:00 PM.
  5. Save your application/confirmation details for future reference.

Background

The CPO exam is SSC's recruitment gateway for Sub-Inspector posts in the Delhi Police executive cadre and the Central Armed Police Forces. Under Advt. E/37/2026-C-2, the examination date has not been announced yet — updates will appear on the official SSC portal.
