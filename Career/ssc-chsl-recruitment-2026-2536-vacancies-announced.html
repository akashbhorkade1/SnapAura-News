SSC CHSL 2026: 2,536 Vacancies — Eligibility & Last Date

SnapAura Career Desk – 2026-09-09

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) has opened the Combined Higher Secondary Level (10+2) Examination 2026 for 2,536 vacancies — Data Entry Operator (DEO), DEO Grade 'A', Junior Department Clerk (LDC) and Junior Secretarial Assistant (JSA). 12th-pass candidates can apply until 7 October 2026, 11:00 PM.

🏢 OrganizationStaff Selection Commission (SSC)
💼 PostsDEO, DEO Grade 'A', LDC, JSA
🔢 Vacancies2,536
🎓 Qualification12th pass (Science+Maths for DEO)
🎂 Age Limit18–27 years (as on 01-08-2026)
💰 Application Fee₹100 (Gen/OBC); women & reserved exempt
📅 Last Date7 October 2026, 11:00 PM
📍 Job LocationAll India

Can I Apply? 👀

Per Majhi Naukri's report of the SSC notification (Advt. HQ-C1102/5/2026-C-1):

🪫 DEO / DEO Grade 'A':12th standard pass in the Science stream with Mathematics as a compulsory subject.
🪫 LDC / JSA:12th standard pass or equivalent from a recognised Board or University.
🎂 Age:18 to 27 years as on 1 August 2026. SC/ST get 5 years' relaxation, OBC 3 years.

If you've passed 12th standard (Science with Maths for DEO) and you're 18–27 as on 1 August 2026, you can apply online before 7 October.

  • 2,536 vacancies across central government departments
  • DEO, DEO Grade 'A', LDC and JSA roles
  • No fee for women, SC/ST, PWD and Ex-Servicemen
  • Selection through the standard Tier-I / Tier-II exam structure

What should I do now? 🚀

  1. Check you match the qualification for DEO or LDC/JSA posts.
  2. Read the official notification (PDF) before applying.
  3. Keep your 12th-marksheet, ID and photo/signature scans ready.
  4. Apply on the official SSC portal before 7 October 2026, 11:00 PM.
  5. Save your application/confirmation details for future reference.

Background

CHSL is SSC's national examination for 12th-pass candidates seeking central government clerical and data-entry roles. Under Advt. HQ-C1102/5/2026-C-1, the selection is expected through Tier-I and Tier-II examinations, whose dates will be announced later by the Commission.
