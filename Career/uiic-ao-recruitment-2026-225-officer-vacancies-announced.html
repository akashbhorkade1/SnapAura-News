UIIC AO Recruitment 2026: 225 Vacancies

SnapAura Career Desk – 2026-09-08

United India Insurance Company (UIIC) is recruiting 225 Administrative Officers (Scale-I) — 200 Generalist and 25 Hindi Officer posts (Advt. No. HO:HRM:REC:AO:1:2026). Applications opened 8 September and close 28 September 2026.

🏢 OrganizationUnited India Insurance Co. Ltd. (UIIC)
💼 PostsAdministrative Officer (Scale-I) — Generalist & Hindi Officer
🔢 Vacancies225 (Generalist 200, Hindi Officer 25)
🎓 QualificationDegree/PG with 60% marks (55% SC/ST/PWD)
🎂 Age Limit21–30 years (as on 30-06-2026)
💰 Application Fee₹1,000 (₹250 SC/ST/ExSM/women)
📅 Last Date28 September 2026
📍 Job LocationAll India

Can I Apply? 👀

As reported by Majhi Naukri:

🪫 Generalist (200 posts):Bachelor's or Master's degree in any discipline with at least 60% aggregate marks (55% for SC/ST/PWD).
🪫 Hindi Officer (25 posts):Master's degree in Hindi with English as a compulsory/elective subject, with at least 60% marks (55% for SC/ST/PWD).
🎂 Age:21 to 30 years as on 30 June 2026. SC/ST get 5 years' relaxation, OBC 3 years.

If you have the required degree/PG with 60% marks (55% for SC/ST/PWD) and you're 21–30 as on 30 June 2026, apply before 28 September.
