PGIMER Recruitment 2026: 243 Nursing Officer Vacancies

SnapAura Career Desk – 2026-09-10

PGIMER (Postgraduate Institute of Medical Education & Research), Chandigarh, is hiring 243 Nursing Officers (Group B) under Advertisement No. PGI/RC/055/2026/2276. Online applications are open and close on 3 October 2026.

🏢 OrganizationPGIMER, Chandigarh
💼 PostNursing Officer (Group B)
🔢 Vacancies243
🎓 QualificationB.Sc Nursing / GNM + 1-year experience
🎂 Age Limit18–35 years (as on 03-10-2026)
💰 Application Fee₹1,500 (₹800 SC/ST; PWD exempt)
📅 Last Date3 October 2026
📍 Job LocationChandigarh

Can I Apply? 👀

As reported by Majhi Naukri, you must have:

🎓 Education:B.Sc. (Hons) Nursing, B.Sc. (Nursing), or a GNM diploma.
🏥 Experience:Minimum 1 year in a hospital with at least 50 beds.
🎂 Age:18 to 35 years as on 3 October 2026. SC/ST get 5 years' relaxation, OBC 3 years.

If you have the required nursing qualification, the 50+ bed hospital experience, and you're within the age limit, you can go ahead and check the application process.
